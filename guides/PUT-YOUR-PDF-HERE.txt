DROP THE GUIDE PDF IN THIS FOLDER
=================================

The site expects one file here:

    guides/blood-pressure-turnaround.pdf

If your PDF has a different name, you have two options:

  A) Rename your file to  blood-pressure-turnaround.pdf   (easiest), or
  B) Open index.html, find the line:

         data-file="guides/blood-pressure-turnaround.pdf"

     and change it to your actual filename.

Until a real file is sitting here, clicking "Download the guide" will
try to fetch a file that doesn't exist and the browser will 404.
Test it once locally before you deploy.

You can delete this .txt file once the PDF is in place.
